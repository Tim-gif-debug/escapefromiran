Escape From Iran: Support

Escape From Iran is a story game in fourteen chapters. It follows one Iranian family from 1933 to the present, written by Alex Grand from his family’s records.

How to play

Tap to move through each scene. When a choice appears, tap the one you want. The short games inside the story each show their own instruction at the bottom of the screen.

Continuing a story

The app saves your place by itself. Open it and tap Continue. The round button at the top right opens the chapter list.

Sound

If there is no music, check that the ring switch on the side of the iPhone is not set to silent and that the volume is up.
