Escape From Iran: Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026

Escape From Iran is published by Comic Book Historians, LLC.

What the app collects

The app collects no personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not connect to the internet while it runs.

What stays on the device

The app saves the chapter and scene last reached, so the story can be continued later. This is stored only on the device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions can be sent to Comicbookhistorians@gmail.com.
